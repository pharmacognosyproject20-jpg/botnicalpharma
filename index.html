About Botanical Pharma

Botanical Pharma is an educational digital platform developed to organize information about selected Pakistani medicinal plants. It brings together botanical taxonomy, phytochemical screening, marker compounds and TLC fingerprint information.

Our vision: Connecting botanical knowledge with digital technology to support medicinal plant education and research.
